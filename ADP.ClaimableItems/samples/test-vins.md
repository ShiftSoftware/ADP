# Test VINs for the service contract samples

VINs whose model has a **contract-type menu** in the local Cosmos emulator's data, for trying the samples' contract
form. They come from a snapshot of the emulator taken on 2026-11-01, so a VIN may stop working if the emulator is
reseeded.

## How they were found

The vehicle lookup joins a vehicle to its menu by the **basic model code**: the Katashiki's first segment, with a
trailing `L` or `R` dropped when it is longer than five characters (`AXVA70L-AEZSBV` → `AXVA70`). Each model below has:

- a menu variant with `MenuType` = Contract (2) in `Services/ServiceMenus`, with its periods, labour and items;
- vehicles in `CompanyData/Vehicles` (`VehicleEntry`) whose Katashiki maps to it.

## Testing notes

- **Mileage:** a periodic service at or below the entered mileage is not offered. A low mileage (say 1,000 km)
  shows the most lines.
- **One contract per periodic service:** once a contract is issued, its periodic services are taken for that VIN
  until they are cancelled or expire. To start clean, use another VIN of the same model.
- **Unpriced parts:** a line whose parts have no price for the country is shown as unavailable.
- **Countries:** SingleCountry prices with no country (country 0); MultiCountry asks for a country and a transfer
  rate.
