About this page

Paint readings for the VIN, one tab per inspection (source on top, date beneath). Each row is a body panel with its left and right reading; a panel with one reading (roof, hood, tail gate) puts it in the Left column with a grey Centre chip. The photo button on a row opens that row's pictures under it, one row at a time; All photos at the foot of the panel shows every picture of the inspection. A row's drawer starts shut and requests no picture until it is opened; All photos starts expanded (its pictures load lazily as they scroll into view) and the reader can fold it, which survives a lookup.

Open a drawer, then look up another vehicle: it stays open, goes under the skeleton with its row and shows the new vehicle's pictures on the answer. It shuts only if the new answer has none for that row. Switching a tab fades the values instead (nothing is fetched).

Each fixture chip says which state it reaches. Empty clears the panel back to idle and Error raises one on a settled card. The certificate sits in the panel's head, before the verdict pill; it says when there is none rather than disappearing: reach it with the pre-owned fixture. Switch the Environment control in the rail for the no-photo and some-photo vehicles. There is no skipped state: a wrapper never skips this panel's lookup.

The controls above the panel are what a host sets on the element: hidden-fields (inspectionDate, source, photos, certificate), show-reading-summary (off by default: one quiet line between the tabs and the table) and show-certificate-button.
