adp-web-components · vehicle lookup

About this page

Who sold the vehicle and when: the selling dealer and branch in the head, then the invoice, the dates, the broker and the end customer below it — every field the lookup carries, each shown exactly as it was sent.

Supply chain turns on show-supply-chain: the same record read as the road the vehicle took — distributor, intermediaries, dealer, broker, end customer — each leg with its own invoice number and date. A leg the record does not have is simply not drawn. Off, the panel still shows all of it in its standard layout.

A missing end customer is missing data, not a verdict: the host may not look customers up, the customer may not be in its records, or a broker has not completed its invoice yet. The one thing the panel does derive is broker stock — a broker with no invoice holds the vehicle, and it says so.

Each fixture chip says which state it reaches. Empty clears the panel back to idle and Error raises one on a settled card. Switch the Environment control in the rail to reach another environment's vehicles. The page starts on Show all so every field is visible; Host default puts back the fields a host hides unless it says otherwise.

This page deliberately consumes the component the way a Blazor host does — through setBlazorRef and callbacks named as attribute strings — rather than by assigning functions to the props.
