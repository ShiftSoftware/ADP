Booking calendar

Usage & examples

Each example is live, with the code beside it. The appearance switch applies to all of them.

1 · Playground

Every prop and the event log, in its own page.

2 · States

Switch the state: the box keeps its size in every state and view. This one opts in to the label and the status line (show-label, show-status); both are off by default.

3 · A 12-hour clock

Each open day shows how many times it has (amber when three or fewer are left); hover or focus a day for its hours. Times read 9:00 AM. The default has no label and no status line.

4 · Standalone

No form: listen for slotChange and send its value.

5 · Host override

A few --shift-* variables reach the calendar and the times; ::part reaches the field.

6 · In a shift-input

Put the calendar in the picker slot of a shift-input: the field opens it in its popover, shows the picked slot, submits its value (ISO with an offset), closes on a time and clears when the branch changes. Each day shows its count of times; hover or focus a day for its hours. This data has days with many times; the list scrolls inside the box.

7 · Your own input

A read-only input opens a shift-popover with trigger="anchor" and placement="top". The calendar owns the value: it reports the label of the value it was given (kept even when it is not a time, like this past one), writes value-format, clears only when the branch changes, and the popover closes when a time is picked.

8 · No available slots

A branch with no times keeps its month on screen, every day greyed, under a message; month navigation still works. show-empty-state="false" turns the message off.
